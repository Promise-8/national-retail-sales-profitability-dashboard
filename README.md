# National Retail Co. _ Sales & Profitability Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Power BI dashboard developed
to analyze sales performance, profitability, customer segments,
product categories, and regional performance for National Retail Co.

The dashboard is designed to support management decision-making
through interactive KPIs and visual analysis.

## Key Analysis Areas

- Sales performance over time
- Regional sales performance
- Product category performance
- Sub-category profitability
- Customer segment contribution
- Regional and category profitability

## Key Findings

### 1. Furniture has weak profitability

Furniture generated approximately $742K in sales but only
$18.45K in profit, resulting in a margin of about 2.5%.

### 2. Technology is the strongest profit contributor

Technology generated more than $145K in profit and was the
strongest category in terms of profitability.

### 3. West leads regional sales

The West region generated the highest total sales, followed
by East, Central, and South.

### 4. Consumer segment dominates revenue

The Consumer segment contributes approximately half of total
revenue, while Corporate and Home Office contribute smaller
shares.

### 5. Sales show seasonal patterns

Sales increase around September and October, suggesting an
opportunity for seasonal inventory and marketing planning.

## Business Recommendations

- Review Furniture pricing and discounting strategies.
- Investigate the causes of weak Furniture profitability in Central.
- Increase investment in high-performing Technology products.
- Develop strategies to grow Corporate and Home Office customers.
- Prepare inventory and marketing campaigns before seasonal peaks.
- Investigate the practices driving strong performance in the West.
